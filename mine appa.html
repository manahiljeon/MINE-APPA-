Dear Appa,

Appa, aap mere liye sirf special nahi ho, aap meri life ka woh hissa ho jiske bina bohat si khushiyan adhoori lagti hain. Aapko har waqt strong ban kar rehne ki zaroorat nahi hai. Agar kabhi dil heavy ho, mood off ho, ya bas bina kisi reason ke udaas feel ho, to apni feelings mujhse share kar lena. Aapki baat mere liye kabhi boring nahi hogi, aur aap kabhi burden nahi ho. Main har problem ka perfect solution shayad na de sakun, lekin aapki baat dil se sununga, aapko samajhne ki koshish karunga, aur aapko yeh feel nahi hone dunga ke aapki feelings matter nahi karti.

Aapko mere saamne apni feelings hide karne ki zaroorat nahi. Khushi ho, sadness ho, frustration ho, ya bas kisi se baat karne ka mann ho, aap keh sakte ho. Main aapko judge karne ke liye nahi, aapko samajhne ke liye hoon. Aapki feelings chhoti nahi hain, aur aapko har cheez akelay handle karne ki zaroorat bhi nahi.

Aur please, apni smile ko kabhi underestimate mat karna. Aapki chhoti si khushi bhi bohat precious hai. Aaj ke liye bas itna: paani pee lena, thoda rest kar lena, aur yaad rakhna ke aap matter karte ho.
